From Texas to Alexandra

Our Story

Siga Culinary was founded by Gift Sedibeng, a chef and entrepreneur from Alexandra Township. His culinary journey was influenced by an opportunity to travel and work in Texas in the United States.During his time in Texas, Gift developed a strong interest in Mexican and Tex-Mex cuisine. When he returned to South Africa, he saw an opportunity to introduce these exciting flavours to his own community. Instead of simply opening a tranditional Mexican restaurant, he created something that reflected both his international experience and his township roots. This led to the creation of Siga Culinary and it's unique Mexi-Kasi identity. Today,Siga Culinary represents creativity, resilience, entrepreneurship and a belief that food can create opportunities for people and communities. The business has also been involved in culinary training. Reporting in 2025 noted that Siga had trained students through a culinary school, with some trainees going on to work as chefs in Johannesburg restaurants.Siga has also participated in community-focused culinary activities. In May 2025, the restaurant hosted an event where young children from Alexandra Early Childhood Development centres participated in cooking and food-presentation activities.

Mission

Our mission is to create memorable food experiences by bringing together Mexican-inspired cuisine and South African township culture. We aim to use food as a way of encouraging creativity, celebrating culture and bringing people together.

Our Vision

Our vision is to continue developing Siga Culinary as a distinctive South African food brand that represents innovation, cultural fusion and the creativity of township cuisine, while creating opportunities for aspiring chefs and members of the community.

What is Mexi-Kasi?

Mexi-Kasi is unique culinary identity of Siga Culinary. It combines the bold flavours of Mexican and Tex-Mex cuisine with familier South African township food.

Mexican Inspiration

Mexican-inspired flavours such as tacos, nachos, quecamole bring bold and exciting flavours to the menu.

Kasi Culture

Township favouries and familiar local food traditions are reimagined and combined with international culinary ideas.

Creative Fusion

The result is a unique food experience that celebrates both local identity and global inspiration.
