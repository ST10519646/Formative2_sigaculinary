Siga Culinary Experience Comes To Alexandra

AUGUST 2026

Celebrating Township Food And Local Talent

Siga Culinary recently brought together food, culture and entrepreneurship through the Culinary Experience in East Bank, Alexandra.

The event celebrated some of Gauteng's exciting township food businesses and created an opportunity for visitors to experience the creativity and diversity of local food culture.

The Culinary Experience included food, music, community interaction and a variety of local businesses. The event represents Siga Culinary's passion for creating opportunities that support township entrepreneurs and bring people together.

SIGA STORY

The Story Behind Mexi-Kasi

Mexi-Kasi is the unique food identity behind Siga Culinary. It brings together Mexican and Tex-Mex flavours with familiar South African township food culture.

Inspired by Chef Gift Sedibeng's culinary experience in Texas, the concept was created to introduce exciting new flavours while still celebrating local kasi culture.

FOOD & FLAVOUR

The Famous Kota Burrito

One of Siga Culinary's most recognised creations is the Kota Burrito, a creative combination of a township favourite and Mexican-inspired flavours.

The dish represents the heart of the Mexi-Kasi concept by combining familiar local ingredients with bold flavours such as salsa and guacamole.

The Vision Behind Siga Culinary

Siga Culinary was created from Chef Gift Sedibeng's passion for food, creativity and his belief in the potential of township entrepreneurship.

His journey from Alexandra to Texas and back helped inspire the unique Mexi-Kasi food concept that continues to make Siga Culinary stand out.
